Why Fomoji

Most software makes you create a separate account for every tool. Fomoji is built to make that unnecessary — one identity, created once, usable everywhere it's supported.

Built for connected software

Fomoji sits underneath the ecosystem, not inside any one app in it.

Privacy should be understandable

Different AI providers handle your data differently. Once AI connection modes exist, Fomoji's job is to make those differences visible instead of hiding them behind one blanket privacy promise.

Our direction

Fomoji is being built as a simple connection layer for an ecosystem of software, AI tools, and developer experiences — create once, connect easily, choose how your AI works.
